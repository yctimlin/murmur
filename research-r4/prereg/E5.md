## Metrics
- Quality q: blind judge overall (RUBRIC.md). Collapse: q < 3.0 or not delivered.
- Speed: active_nf (freeze-excluded active minutes, r4eval.py, as E4). murmur's run.json active_seconds and the
  runner's clock.active_min are reported as cross-checks.
- Pairs: (task, rep k) pairs K1F vs base4F. d_q = q(K1F) - q(base4F); r = ln(active_nf(K1F)/active_nf(base4F)).
  90% two-sided t-intervals over pairs. Tokens reported, not gating (AMENDMENT-5).

## Decision rule (tools: analysis/e5_decide.py, written before the block-1 interim look)
Interim after block 1 (n = 4 pairs) and block 2 (n = 8):
- STOP-REJECT (keep base4F) if any: upper 90% CI of mean d_q < 0; lower 90% CI of mean r > 0 (K1F clearly slower);
  collapses(K1F) - collapses(base4F) >= 2.
- STOP-KEEP, only at block 2: mean d_q >= 0 AND lower 90% CI of d_q >= -0.5 AND collapses(K1F) <= collapses(base4F)
  AND upper 90% CI of mean r <= 0.
- Otherwise run the next block.
Final after block 3 (n = 12): KEEP K1F iff mean d_q >= 0 AND lower 90% CI of d_q >= -1.0 AND collapses(K1F) <=
collapses(base4F) AND exp(mean r) <= 1.00 (not slower). Else keep base4F (current murmur).
A pair whose run is still infra after 3 reruns is dropped from the pairs (reported).
