## Decision rules
- D1 (M0 executes): in K0+K1 runs, S1 premature-END episodes (team END within 10 active min after a time-pressure post
  made before budget minute 90) in <= 2 of 16 runs, and S2 early quits = 0. If D1 fails, M0 is fixed and E4 re-run
  under a new amendment; no K is a FINAL candidate meanwhile.
- D2 (K0 non-regression vs base4): mean over the 4 tasks of [mean K0 quality − base4 quality] >= −0.3 AND collapse
  rate K0 <= collapse rate base4 (E4 runs; E1 base4 runs reported as secondary).
- D3 (KEEP K1 over K0): mean over 8 (task, rep) pairs of quality(K1) − quality(K0) >= +0.3 AND collapses(K1) <=
  collapses(K0) AND geometric mean per-task active_nf ratio K1/K0 <= 1.25.
- Safety stop: if >= 3 of the 4 block-1 K1 runs collapse, the block-2 K1 runs are withdrawn and K1 is REJECTED.
- Reported, not gating: active_nf vs solo per arm (the FINAL's G3 decides speed), tokens, paired 90% t-intervals.
