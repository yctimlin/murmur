## Validation and unproven claims

At phase-D starting revision `8f77689`, a3 ran `.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q`: **39 passed in 11.29s**. The [test and noise audit record](research/notebook/20261017-1430-a3-final-noise-and-suite.md) records this run; independent artifact reviews are linked from the final report. The source/tests match the tested bug-fix base; the null candidate adds no runtime mechanism. The research includes deterministic analysis tooling, raw scheduler results, immutable preregistrations, and append-only review notebooks.

Unproven: quality on genuinely long tasks; protocol versus parallel-work causes of historical quality loss; the benefit and token cost of a third peer; bug-fix noninferiority, rare failure prevention and real-provider retry effectiveness; the amount of cached input that can safely be removed; and transport of these short-task noise estimates to a sealed evaluation. Future sizing must use independently calibrated tasks and allow for pilot uncertainty; the observed score SD0 must never imply zero required replication. No behavioral improvement is promoted from these null results.
