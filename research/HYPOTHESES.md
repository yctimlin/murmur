# Falsifiable hypotheses registered before batch C1

The numeric model and its elicited ranges are in `MODEL.md`; the executable design and analysis rules are in `prereg/C1.json`. Hypotheses are working beliefs, not accepted findings. `n` refers to independent task runs, not hidden-test cases or repeated judges. H1–H4 are addressed by C1; H5–H6 are scheduled for C2 and cannot be confirmed by C1. A missed predicted range challenges that prediction; a wide confidence interval means inconclusive, not “no effect.”

C1 has three independent original comparisons and three A/A contrasts per task. A two-sided sign test with three nonzero contrasts cannot reach p<.05 (smallest p=.25). Six clean base3 runs do not prove equivalence or rule out rare failures. No multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory discovery or mechanism promotion is claimed from these screens. Any exclusions, failed/capped deliveries, rate limiting and missing telemetry remain visible. C1.json specifies authoritative numerical estimators and inclusion rules; if a discrepancy is found before execution, correct it with a new notebook entry and coordinated revision, never by editing a started preregistration.
