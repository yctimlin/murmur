# C2 analysis: workflow_store exec-only calibration (a1, 2026-10-17)

## Question
Does the preregistered `workflow_store` task satisfy the operator admission gate for any swarm jobs, and how did the three identical-treatment calibration runs compare with their frozen forecasts?

## Method and preregistration
I analyzed every result copied to `research/data/raw/C2/`, using the immutable job map in [`jobs.json`](../data/raw/C2/jobs.json) and the preregistration [`C2.json`](../prereg/C2.json). The three runs share block `b00-workflow-calibration`; pairwise differences are descriptive same-treatment A/A noise, not independent treatment contrasts. I used arithmetic means and sample SD (n−1), min/max, CV for positive metrics, and the preregistered signed errors/MAE, mean log(observed/forecast), and per-run prediction-band coverage. No run was removed for outcome, and no significance or equivalence test was added.

## Forecast and decision rule

Frozen point forecasts were score .80, active 45 min, input 2,700,000 and uncached 162,000. Observed means therefore have signed errors (observed minus forecast) of +0.20 score, −28.4733 min, −1,674,155 input, and −94,107 uncached. Score MAE is .20; active/input/uncached MAE equal the absolute signed errors. Mean log errors are respectively +0.223144 (score, descriptive only), −1.011529, −0.977215, and −0.875165 for active/input/uncached. Per-run bands cover score 3/3 ([.55,1.0]), active 2/3 ([15,90]), input 3/3 ([600,000,9,000,000]), and uncached 3/3 ([30,000,720,000]). The active miss is the 14.31-minute run; its value is retained.

The conservative preregistered admission rule requires **each** run to have score rate ≤.85 and active time ≥30 minutes, plus valid telemetry, delivery, no leak, and no cap/timeout. Validity/delivery/leak requirements pass, but all three score rates are 1.0 (> .85) and all active times are below 30 minutes. Therefore `workflow_store` is ineligible for swarm use. The operator gate independently fails on both dimensions. The task's short/easy behavior cannot establish that another redesign would meet the gate.

## Predictions and decision

The calibration prediction that this task would be unsaturated and at least 30 minutes did not hold: all three scores saturated at 1.0 and active times were 14.31–19.81 minutes. The broad input and uncached bands held; the active band held for 2/3 runs. This is a calibration result, not evidence of a quality improvement or lower murmur cost. No mechanism or peer-count/default change is selected. C2's preregistered ineligible branch requires finishing the round in `research/C-DONE.md`; the remaining 15 jobs and approximately 348,370,207 input-token budget by preregistration estimate (raw C1+C2 arithmetic leaves 354,292,672) are unspent, while protocol-versus-parallelism, peer-count scaling, unsaturated quality noise, and bug-fix noninferiority remain unanswered.
