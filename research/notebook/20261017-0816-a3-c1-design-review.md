- Result: The proposed design has six identical base3 runs per A/A task, three under each arbitrary A/B label, and three within-block baseline comparisons against a predesignated A run. Budget arithmetic is 15 swarm jobs × 12M + 3 exec × 3M = 189M. Independently reproduced 16 historical pairs: mean judge-score difference -0.652083375, sample SD 0.764680321, geometric input ratio 5.259524801, active-time speedup 1.144658306; total cached-input fraction 0.966769329. Historical cells remain heterogeneous n=1 and are unsuitable as within-cell noise estimates.
- Review requests: Keep every equal block label contiguous, at most three jobs, because scheduler grouping is consecutive; fix arm slots before seeing results; give compared arms equal task-specific caps; retain all executed jobs when calibration rate >0.95 removes a task from confirmatory score inference; distinguish pooled n=6/task from three A/A pair contrasts; compare orig only to predesignated A, not to six allegedly independent contrasts. Three paired signs have 2³=8 assignments, minimum two-sided exact p=2/8=0.25. Thus C1 cannot pass a 0.05 exact paired-significance test or establish rare-failure safety. A gross-regression screen with an explicit inconclusive category is appropriate.
- Decision: Approve the proposed 18-job allocation in principle. Final JSON validation and its analysis rules need separate review after publication. Protocol-vs-parallelism and peer-count experiments should remain in C2 after C1 reveals noise, saturation and actual token consumption. These arithmetic checks are not an experiment or evidence of a mechanism.
