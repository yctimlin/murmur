# C1 preregistration: noise before protocol attribution

- **Question:** How variable is unchanged base3 on a repeated task, and do its bug fixes show a gross regression relative to protected eec78f8? Can the frozen screening tasks avoid a ceiling under plain exec?
- **Method:** Freeze `research/prereg/C1.json` before the scheduler runs anything. Eighteen jobs, seven consecutive concurrent blocks, no more than three jobs per block. The first block has one exec calibration on each frozen task. The remaining blocks alternate ledger_replay and reserveplan. Each has three A/A pairs: six unchanged base3 runs/task, with three runs under each arbitrary A/B label. Ledger blocks each also contain one original-baseline run. Original compares only with that block's predesignated base3_A. All ledger runs use 60 minutes and all reserveplan runs 90 minutes; the document calibration uses 60 minutes. A/B names change no prompt, revision, settings, peer count or cap. Slot order rotates as recorded in JSON.
- **Prediction:** Frozen model means and marginal subjective 80% individual-run intervals are in JSON and MODEL.md. Same-treatment A/A expected score difference is zero and time/input/uncached ratios are one. Within-task base3 dispersion priors are score SD .10 (range .03–.20), active CV .20 (.10–.40), input CV .35 (.15–.70), uncached CV .40 (.15–.80). Bug-fix prior is zero score change and unit cost ratios; a .8–1.2 effect-ratio range is a subjective prior, not the practical decision margin.
- **Raw-data path:** Future `research/data/raw/C1/jobs.json` and `research/data/raw/C1/C1-*.json`, plus `/workspace/self3/labdata/results/<name>/` with result, setup/run/score logs, delivered tree and ws sessions. These do not yet exist. No jobs were submitted or candidates run by this researcher.
- **Result:** Pre-data design and read-only validation only; experimental outcomes are pending. `python3 research/tools/validate_c1.py` verifies exactly 18 jobs, seven contiguous blocks sized 3/3/2/3/2/3/2, six base3 replicates each on ledger_replay and reserveplan, three original-baseline comparisons, equal A/A treatments, valid caps, and both revisions in r3.git. Its result is retained in `research/data/C1-design-validation.json`.
- **Decision:** Run only this preregistered batch through the scheduler after phase B. Defer protocol and peer-count attribution to C2 after noise and calibration are known. C2 is not preregistered here and must receive a fresh preregistration before execution.

## Calibration, exclusions and retained failures

A calibration `score.rate > 0.95` makes that task ineligible for confirmatory score/noise/effect inference; exactly .95 is retained. All already scheduled work remains in the raw data and descriptive cost/outcome tables. The scheduler's batch is static: calibration does not secretly cancel, replace or retune subsequent jobs. If a task saturates, C1's confirmatory sample shrinks and C2 needs an eligible task or preregistered recalibration. If both A/A tasks saturate, C1 cannot establish unsaturated score-noise or bug-fix conclusions. If reserveplan saturates, the planned long-task protocol test is blocked until a suitable frozen long task is available; never silently use a short task.

A missing/invalid/leaked calibration is inconclusive, not a passing suitability test. Runs with `leak_hits > 0` are excluded from confirmatory contrasts and retained as flagged descriptive rows, with a full scheduled-set sensitivity. There are no unregistered replacement runs. Missing telemetry is missing, not zero; a null mutation score is missing/undefined, not zero quality. Nondelivery remains delivery=0 with its observed valid score and costs. A timeout/cap is retained and marks time-to-delivery right censoring. Do not obtain apparent speed or cost noninferiority by dropping failed/capped runs.

## Analysis and decisions frozen before data

Report every run, scheduled and eligible counts, task/arm mean and sample SD, and the task's pooled six-run base3 mean/SD. Never pool task scores as if tasks were interchangeable. Report each A-minus-B score contrast, log(A/B) for positive active/input/uncached measures, corresponding geometric mean ratios and delivery discordance. Arithmetic raw means/SD remain visible. Zero or missing costs preclude that logarithmic comparison, without zero imputation.

For ledger bug-fix comparisons use only A-minus-original in the three preregistered blocks. A practical gross-regression screen triggers on any A nondelivery while original delivers, a newly confirmed P0 with runnable reproducer, mean score loss greater than .05, or geometric mean active/input/uncached ratio greater than 1.25. A trigger calls for diagnosis and preregistered follow-up, not a causal declaration from three runs.

A provisional score noninferiority statement requires the one-sided paired-t 95% lower bound `mean(diff) - 2.919986*sd(diff)/sqrt(3) > -.05`, three complete eligible pairs, and explicit acknowledgment of the normality assumption. Provisional cost noninferiority requires the corresponding upper bound on each of the three mean log ratios below `log(1.25)`, using complete positive uncensored data. All endpoints must pass for a joint provisional no-large-regression statement, and any delivery/P0 screen blocks promotion. Otherwise state **inconclusive**, even if means look favorable. These are conservative gates for measured endpoints, not proof of equivalence or delivery reliability.

Also report assumption-dependent two-sided 95% paired-t intervals (df=2, multiplier 4.302653). With three independent sign-flippable pair contrasts there are only eight permutations and the smallest two-sided exact p is .25. No .05 exact significance claim is possible. Do not invent power through treating hidden-test cases, judges, peers or the shared comparator as independent experimental units. The A/A and bug-fix summaries share the same A runs and are correlated.

Report `lines_429` and wall time by arm/block. Primary analysis includes rate-limited runs, because load and retry behavior can be part of the treatment. A clean-complete-block sensitivity can diagnose operational variation but cannot support causal adjustment with n=3. The ledger triple has nine live peers versus six in reserveplan's A/A pair, so cross-task time differences mix task and load; use within-task comparisons. Three calibration execs also share a block and provide only one realization/task.

Assess out-of-sample forecasts with signed score errors, log ratios for positive time/tokens, mean errors and marginal interval coverage, separately by task/treatment. A task/treatment mean outside its stated individual-run range, or empirical base3 dispersion outside its prior range, falsifies that working numerical prediction. This is a declared calibration diagnostic, not a p-value or calibrated confidence statement. Score forecasting is restricted to eligible tasks, but every executed task remains visible descriptively. Do not revise MODEL.md or C1.json to fit C1 once execution starts; write a new notebook entry/model version instead.
